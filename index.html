Hunted

A 2D horror game concept where the creature remembers where you hid. In two weeks, we built the pitch a publisher would need to sign it.

15players took our concept test
€400Kproduction budget, planned
34,329copies to break even, projected

The goal

Convince a publisher to sign a new game, with a concept tested on players and a credible plan to launch it.

Marketing & Management of Innovation, November 2026.

What we did

  • Found the gap in Steam data and player reviews: story-driven horror games are rarely played twice
  • Tested the concept with 15 players and changed the design from what they missed
  • Costed a €400K production over 16 months and built the financing plan
  • Designed a €138K publisher deal with go/no-go gates, break-even and three scenarios

Team and my part

2 students. Built the whole pitch in a team of two: market research, the player test, the business model, the financing and the deal.

FragZone

A paintball arena built like three Call of Duty maps, an hour from Paris. As CEO of a team of five, I led its business plan and its pitch to investors.

€1.3Mfunding needed at launch
30%IRR over 3 years, base case
−2%IRR if price and clients drop 10%

The goal

Turn a business idea into a business plan solid enough to convince investors to buy shares in the company.

Business creation (management & accounting), November to December 2025.

What we did

  • Sized a fragmented market: 150 to 200 paintball centres in France, with no national brand
  • Set the assumptions: €60 per player and about 47,000 players a year
  • Built a €1.3M funding plan, with €300K of equity offered for 20 to 25% of the company
  • Stress-tested the plan: break-even, sensitivity and three scenarios

Team and my part

5 students. CEO of the team: I split the work and kept the deadlines, worked on the financial model and the scenarios, and led the pitch to the jury.
